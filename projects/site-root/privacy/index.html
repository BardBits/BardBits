Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

BardBits does not ask who you are. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no advertising networks, and no analytics. This site sets no cookies. The short version: the tools run in your browser, and nothing you type or generate is sent to us.

This policy covers bardbits.ca and every project on it. Browser storage belongs to the whole domain rather than to any one page, and the site is run by one person, so a single policy applies throughout. Where a particular project does something different, it is described in its own section below.

What is not collected

  • No accounts, names, email addresses, or payment details.
  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, session recording, or fingerprinting.
  • No advertising networks, and no data sold or rented to anyone.
  • No third-party scripts of any kind. The site enforces this technically as well as by policy: a Content-Security-Policy header blocks the browser from loading scripts, images, or frames from other domains.

Storage in your browser

Some tools remember things between pages using your browser's sessionStorage. This stays on your device, is readable only by bardbits.ca, is never transmitted to any server, and is erased by your browser when you close the tab.

Name generators

The cottage, cabin, and beach house name generators keep the list of names you have already drawn, so it survives moving between them. It is stored under keys beginning retreat-names:history:, holds only generated names, and is discarded when the tab closes. Clearing site data in your browser removes it immediately.

Server logs

The site is served through Amazon CloudFront, which records a line for each request. These logs are used to see which pages are being requested, what is failing, and what is slow. They are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically.

Each entry records:

  • Date, time, and the edge location that served the request
  • The page requested, the HTTP method, and the response status and size
  • How long the request took
  • Your browser's user-agent string and the page that referred you, if any
  • The country the request came from

Visitor IP addresses are deliberately not recorded. The logging configuration omits the IP address field and the cookie field, so the logs cannot be tied back to an individual visitor. This was a choice rather than an oversight: the questions these logs answer do not require knowing who anyone is.

Third parties

Hosting

The site is hosted on Amazon Web Services (S3, CloudFront, and Route 53). AWS processes requests in order to deliver the site and stores the logs described above on our behalf. It does not use that data for its own purposes.

Affiliate links

Some pages link to products on other websites. These are affiliate links: if you follow one and buy something, this site may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Where such links appear, they are disclosed on the page itself.

Affiliate links to Etsy are administered by Rakuten Advertising. Following one takes you to a site run by someone else, and from that point their privacy policy applies, not this one. They commonly set cookies on their own domains to attribute a purchase to the referring site. Nothing identifying you is passed to them by BardBits; the link itself carries only a code identifying this site as the referrer.

Your choices and rights

Because no accounts exist and no personal data is collected, there is very little held about you to begin with. Nothing in the server logs identifies an individual, so a request to access or delete "your data" generally cannot be matched to a person, and the logs expire on their own within 30 days.

Browser storage is entirely under your control: clearing site data, or simply closing the tab, removes it. Depending on where you live you may have rights under laws such as PIPEDA in Canada, the GDPR in the UK and EU, or the CCPA in California. If you would like to exercise one, or want to know what is held, get in touch using the address below.

Children

These tools are suitable for all ages, but they are not directed at children, and no information is knowingly collected from anyone.

Changes

If the way this site handles data changes — for example if a project begins using something not described here — this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy on this site, can be sent to bardbits.ca+privacy@gmail.com.
