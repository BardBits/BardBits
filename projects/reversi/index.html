Reversi

The classic disc-flipping strategy game, free in your browser with nothing to install. Place a disc so it traps a line of your opponent's between two of yours, and every disc in that line turns your colour. Finish with more of the board than they have. Three difficulty levels, and the hardest one plays the last dozen squares out exactly rather than guessing at them.
